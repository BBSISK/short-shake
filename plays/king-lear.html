Shakespeare, in a hurry

King Lear

A king splits his kingdom by asking his daughters who loves him most. It goes badly.

The plot in 30 seconds

Lear hands out land to the daughters who flatter him and banishes the one who tells the truth. The flatterers throw him out, a storm hits, and a parallel plot of forged letters and betrayed sons runs alongside. Nobody learns anything in time.

Who is who

  • Lear: king, asks for flattery, regrets it
  • Goneril & Regan: the flattering daughters
  • Cordelia: the honest daughter, banished
  • Gloucester, Edgar & Edmund: a second family falling apart, with a forged letter at the centre

Words you already use

"More sinned against than sinning"

Lear says this about himself on the heath. We still use it for someone who has been wronged more than they have wronged.

"Every inch a king"

Also Lear, in the storm and madness. Today it is used about anyone who looks the part.

Background

Written around 1605-06. It is one of the grimmest tragedies, and for a long stretch of history a rewritten version with a happy ending was staged instead of the original.
