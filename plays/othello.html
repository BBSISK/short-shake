SHORT SHAKE

Shakespeare, in a hurry

Othello

A soldier is passed over for a promotion. He decides to make his boss doubt his own wife.

A note: Othello deals with racism, jealousy and violence against a wife. Our version keeps the focus on Iago's manipulation, and does not joke about Othello or about the killing.

The plot in 30 seconds

Iago is passed over for promotion, so he persuades Othello that Desdemona is cheating with Cassio, using a lost handkerchief as proof. The lie works. Emilia finally exposes Iago, but too late.

Who is who

  • Othello: general, trusts the wrong man
  • Iago: known as "honest Iago", the villain
  • Desdemona: Othello's wife, innocent
  • Cassio: the new lieutenant, and the one who gets framed
  • Emilia: Iago's wife, who tells the truth in the end

Words you already use

"The green-eyed monster"

Iago warns Othello about jealousy in Act 3, Scene 3, right as he is feeding it.

"Wear my heart upon my sleeve"

Iago, Act 1, Scene 1. The Oxford English Dictionary cites this as the first occurrence of the expression.

Background

Written in the early 1600s, and set in Venice and then Cyprus.
