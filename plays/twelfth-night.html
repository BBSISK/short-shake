Shakespeare, in a hurry

Twelfth Night

A shipwrecked woman dresses as a man. Three people fall in love with the wrong person.

The plot in 30 seconds

After a shipwreck, Viola disguises herself as Cesario and works for Duke Orsino, who loves Olivia, who falls for Cesario. Meanwhile Olivia's staff trick Malvolio with a forged love letter. Viola's twin brother Sebastian arrives, mistakes are untangled, and nearly everyone ends up with a partner.

Who is who

  • Viola: shipwrecked, disguised as Cesario
  • Orsino: duke, in love with the idea of Olivia
  • Olivia: countess, falls for the wrong person
  • Sebastian: Viola's twin, who arrives at just the wrong time
  • Malvolio: stuffy steward, target of the prank

Words you already use

"If music be the food of love, play on"

The play's first line, spoken by Orsino (Act 1, Scene 1).

"Some are born great, some achieve greatness, and some have greatness thrust upon 'em"

Malvolio reads it in the forged letter (Act 2, Scene 5).

"Journeys end in lovers meeting"

From Feste's song in Act 2, Scene 3.

Background

Believed to have been written around 1601-1602, and named after the Twelfth Night holiday that ends the Christmas season.
