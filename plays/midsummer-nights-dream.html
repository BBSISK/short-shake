SHORT SHAKE

Shakespeare, in a hurry

A Midsummer Night's Dream

Four lovers, one forest, one badly aimed love potion, and a man with a donkey head.

The plot in 30 seconds

Hermia loves Lysander, but her father wants her to marry Demetrius, so the pair run into the forest, and Helena and Demetrius follow. The fairy king Oberon sends Puck to fetch a love potion, and Puck uses it on the wrong man. Titania, the fairy queen, falls for Bottom, who has been given a donkey's head. Oberon sorts most of it out, and the story ends with three weddings and a very bad play.

Who is who

  • Hermia & Lysander: in love, on the run
  • Demetrius & Helena: he wants Hermia, she wants him
  • Oberon & Titania: fairy king and queen, not speaking
  • Puck: Oberon's helper, very bad at instructions
  • Bottom: an actor who gets a donkey's head

Words you already use

"The course of true love never did run smooth"

Lysander says it to Hermia in Act 1, Scene 1.

"Lord, what fools these mortals be!"

Puck, watching the lovers in the forest (Act 3, Scene 2).

"Love looks not with the eyes but with the mind"

Helena, in Act 1, Scene 1.

Background

Probably written between 1594 and 1596. It is set in Athens and in a forest outside the city.
