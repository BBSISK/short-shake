Shakespeare, in a hurry

Hamlet

A prince is told by a ghost that his uncle killed his dad. He thinks about it for most of the play.

The plot in 30 seconds

Hamlet's father's ghost says Claudius poisoned him. Hamlet plans revenge, stages a play to test Claudius, swaps a letter that was meant to get him killed, and ends up in a duel where almost everyone dies.

Who is who

  • Hamlet: prince, overthinker
  • Claudius: the uncle, now king
  • The Ghost: Hamlet's dad, with a grudge
  • Rosencrantz & Guildenstern: friends who spy on him

Words you already use

"Brevity is the soul of wit"

Polonius says it, ironically while rambling. We use it to mean short and sharp is best.

"Hoist with his own petard"

Hamlet about the plot against him. A petard was a small bomb, so it means blown up by your own scheme.

Background

Written around 1600. It is Shakespeare's longest play, so our two-part version has to be ruthless.
