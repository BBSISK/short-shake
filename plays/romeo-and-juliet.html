Shakespeare, in a hurry

Romeo and Juliet

Two teenagers from feuding families fall in love in one night. The postal service ruins everything.

A note: The play ends with the deaths of two teenagers. Our version keeps it brief and does not show how they die.

The plot in 30 seconds

Romeo, a Montague, crashes a Capulet party and falls for Juliet. A friar marries them in secret, hoping it will end the feud. A street fight gets Romeo banished, and a plan to fake Juliet's death goes wrong when the letter explaining it never arrives.

Who is who

  • Romeo: a Montague, falls in love quickly
  • Juliet: a Capulet, decides faster
  • Friar Laurence: marries them, makes the plan
  • Mercutio & Tybalt: the street fight that changes everything
  • The Nurse: Juliet's confidante, and the only wedding guest

Words you already use

"Wherefore art thou Romeo?"

Juliet says it in Act 2, Scene 2. "Wherefore" meant "why", not "where": she is asking why he has to be a Montague.

"A plague o' both your houses"

Mercutio says it in Act 3, Scene 1, after he has been fatally wounded in the fight. Today it is used to curse two sides in a quarrel.

"Parting is such sweet sorrow"

Juliet again, at the end of the balcony scene in Act 2, Scene 2.

Background

Written in the 1590s and set in Verona, Italy. The Prologue calls the lovers "star-crossed", a phrase we still use.
