SHORT SHAKE

Shakespeare, in a hurry

Much Ado About Nothing

Two people who swear they hate each other get tricked into love. A wedding goes badly wrong.

A note: The plot hinges on a false accusation against Hero. Our version keeps it short and makes the villain's scheme the target of the joke.

The plot in 30 seconds

Soldiers return to Messina and young Claudio falls for Hero. Beatrice and Benedick trade insults, so their friends trick each into believing the other is in love. Meanwhile the villain Don John fakes a scandal that wrecks Claudio and Hero's wedding. A friar's plan, a bumbling Night Watch and some honest confessions put things right.

Who is who

  • Beatrice & Benedick: sharp tongues, secretly a match
  • Claudio & Hero: young couple whose wedding goes wrong
  • Don Pedro: prince, matchmaker
  • Don John: his bitter brother, the villain
  • Dogberry & the Watch: solve the crime almost by accident

Words you already use

"Lady Disdain"

Benedick's nickname for Beatrice in their first exchange (Act 1, Scene 1).

"A merry war"

Leonato's description of Beatrice and Benedick: they never meet without a skirmish of wit.

"Man is a giddy thing"

Benedick near the end, Act 5, Scene 4.

Background

Thought to have been written in 1598 and 1599. It is set in Messina, on the island of Sicily.
