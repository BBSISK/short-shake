Let's do Twelfth Night in two minutes.
Viola and her twin brother Sebastian are on a ship that sinks. Viola is washed up in a country called Illyria. She thinks her brother has drowned.
She needs a job and a safe way to get one. So she dresses as a man, calls herself Cesario, and goes to work for the local Duke, Orsino.
Orsino is in love with Olivia, a countess who has said no to him many times. He sends Cesario to plead his case. Olivia takes one look at Cesario and falls in love with him. Meanwhile, Cesario has fallen for Orsino. So now Orsino loves Olivia, Olivia loves Cesario, and Cesario loves Orsino.
At the same time, in Olivia's house, the staff are annoyed with Malvolio, her serious, stuffy steward. Maria writes a fake love letter, in Olivia's handwriting, telling him to smile a lot and wear yellow stockings. Malvolio believes it and does exactly that. Olivia thinks he has lost his mind, and he is locked up.
Then Sebastian turns up. He is alive, and he looks exactly like Cesario. Olivia mistakes him for Cesario and asks him to marry her. He is confused, but he says yes.
When Olivia, Cesario and Sebastian are all in the same room, everything is explained. The twins are reunited. Orsino realises that Cesario is a woman, and a woman who has been in love with him all along. He proposes to Viola.
Malvolio is let out and doesn't forgive anybody. He swears he'll get his revenge.
The moral: don't trust unsigned letters.
That was Twelfth Night. Shake you later.
