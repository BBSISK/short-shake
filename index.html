Every play in two short videos. Fake phone screens, no voiceover, one real quote.

King Lear

A king splits his kingdom by asking his daughters who loves him most. It goes badly.

The Merchant of Venice

A friend borrows money, the lender wants a pound of flesh, and a lawyer finds a loophole.

Hamlet

A prince is told by a ghost that his uncle killed his dad. He thinks about it for most of the play.

Macbeth

A general is told he will be king. His wife helps him speed things up.

Othello

A soldier is passed over for a promotion. He decides to make his boss doubt his own wife.

Romeo and Juliet

Two teenagers from feuding families fall in love in one night. The postal service ruins everything.

Twelfth Night

A shipwrecked woman dresses as a man. Three people fall in love with the wrong person.

A Midsummer Night's Dream

Four lovers, one forest, one badly aimed love potion, and a man with a donkey head.

Much Ado About Nothing

Two people who swear they hate each other get tricked into love. A wedding goes badly wrong.
